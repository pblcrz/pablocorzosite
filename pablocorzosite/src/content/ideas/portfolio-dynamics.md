Accumulation spreads the cost of a portfolio across time. Rationalization
concentrates it into a single event. As continuation becomes the default,
portfolio cleanup becomes increasingly difficult.

The intervention isn't the portfolio itself but the operating model behind it.
Here I examine the observation, the mechanism, and the organizational response.
