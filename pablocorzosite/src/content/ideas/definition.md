---
title: "Definition-First"
statement: >
  Clarity earns a product its existence. A product should be able to say
  precisely what it is for and who it is for; when it cannot, that absence —
  not a missing feature — is the signal to question whether it should exist at
  all.
status: "emerging"
introducedAt: 2026-07-26
themes:
  - "product-operations"
---

A placeholder for the definition-first argument: that a product's right to exist
is earned by clarity about its purpose and its user, not by momentum, revenue, or
the fact that it already exists. This idea is still being written; it is recorded
here because [[portfolio-dynamics]] extends the same standard to the exit side of
a product's life.
